Meet Prof

Your AI teaching assistant.

Prof is {{brand}}’s own assistant for tutors. Ask in plain words and it drafts the work you’d otherwise spend your evenings on. You check it, change anything you like, and decide what goes out.

  • Makes work from your own material. Homework, quizzes, tests and exams from your books, notes and syllabus, using the pages you choose.
  • Drafts the marking. It reads typed answers, maths working, photos and whiteboard drawings, marks them against your mark scheme and explains each mistake.
  • Plans the course. It sets out a syllabus in order and spreads it week by week up to the exam.
  • Writes original practice papers in the same format as the real exam, each checked a second time.
  • Builds your question bank and flashcards, with a second check that flags anything that looks wrong.
  • Helps with reports. It suggests a comment for the weekly parent report; you edit it and approve.
  • Reads the paperwork. Syllabus PDFs and grade-threshold tables, so mock grades use real boundaries.
