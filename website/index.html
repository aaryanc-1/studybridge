Questions

Is it really free?

Yes. The Free plan is one learner with every feature, for as long as you like. While paid plans are being set up, nobody is charged at all. When they start, you choose whether to upgrade; nothing changes on its own.

Do learners use AI?

No. Prof, the assistant, only works for the tutor. It drafts marks, feedback, plans and reports, and nothing reaches a learner or parent until the tutor has checked and approved it.

Can it mark handwriting and whiteboard working?

Yes. Prof reads photos of written work and whiteboard drawings, tells you what it read them as, and drafts a mark. You confirm or change it.

What can parents see?

Weekly reports you’ve approved, upcoming lessons and due dates, and marks once you’ve given them back. Never messages or working. Parent accounts are free.

Which exams and subjects does it work with?

Any. Add your own subjects and exams (Cambridge, Edexcel, ECZ, CBSE and others) with your own syllabus. {{brand}} never hosts exam-board papers; you use your own copies.

Which devices?

Windows and Mac apps, plus a phone version for iPhone and Android that you add to your home screen. One account works everywhere.

What about my data?

You can download everything that’s yours as a ZIP file, or delete your account, from Settings at any time. We don’t sell data or show ads. See the privacy policy.
