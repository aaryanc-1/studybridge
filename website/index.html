    Questions

    Can I use {{brand}} without a tutor?

    Yes, that’s the student version, in early access now. You’ll get a plan to your exam, lessons, practice papers and mocks, with Prof as your guide. Join the list below and we’ll tell you when it opens.

    Do students use AI?

    With a tutor: no. Prof works for the tutor, and nothing reaches a learner until the tutor approves it. On your own: Prof guides you with hints, never answers handed over, and everything it makes is checked before you see it and can be reported in one tap.

    Are the practice papers real past papers?

    No. They are original papers written to match each real paper’s format: sections, timing, marks and style. Real past papers open from the exam board’s own website. {{brand}} is not affiliated with or endorsed by Cambridge, Pearson Edexcel or the IB.

    My subject or exam isn’t listed.

    Ask for it on the subjects page. The most-requested come next. In the student version you can also upload your own syllabus or course outline, from grade 7 to university, and get a plan from it.

    How old do I need to be?

    From grade 8 you can start on your own. Below grade 8, a parent sets up or approves the account. In some countries the law asks a parent to confirm for anyone under 18, and the app handles that for you.

    Is it free?

    For tutors, the Free plan is one learner with every feature, and nobody is charged while paid plans are being set up. Parent accounts are always free. Student prices will be announced with early access, with a free way to start.

    Which devices?

    Any browser, on a laptop, tablet or phone, plus the desktop app for Windows and Mac for locked-down mocks. One account works everywhere.

    What about my data?

    You can download everything that’s yours, or delete your account, from Settings at any time. We don’t sell data or show ads. See the privacy policy.
