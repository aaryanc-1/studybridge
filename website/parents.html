What you see, and what you don’t

You see

  • A weekly report, approved by their tutor: lessons, marks, time studied and what’s next.
  • Upcoming lessons and due dates, so nothing is a surprise.
  • Marks once they’re given back, with strong and weak topics.
  • Mock grades and the exam countdown.
  • A way to tell them when your child will be away or late, and to keep their contacts and health details up to date.
  • Their portfolio: the work they choose to show you, and pieces their teachers pick.
  • Messages with their teachers and their school’s office, just between you and them.
  • Parents’ meetings to book online, in person or by video.

You never see

  • Their own messages with their teachers, or another parent’s
  • Their answers and drafts, and anything they keep to themselves in their portfolio
  • Anything before their tutor has approved it
  • The exam camera

Your child can see which parents are linked to their account.

Questions from parents

Does it cost anything?

No. Parent accounts are free on every plan.

Does my child use AI?

No. With a tutor, Prof works only for the tutor, who approves everything first. On their own, your child uses lessons, hints and worked solutions written ahead and checked before they see them. There’s no AI chat.

Can I pay for my child?

Yes. You can pay for your child from your own free parent account: ${{studentMonth}} a month, or an exam pass at ${{studentPass}} a month paid once. See pricing.

Can I message their teachers here?

Yes. Open Messages and write to any of your child’s teachers, or their school’s office. Only you and they see what you write, and you’re told when they answer. A school can ask families to write to the office or their child’s form tutor first.

How do parents’ meetings work?

When your child’s school or tutor opens them, you’re told. Pick a time with each of their teachers, in person or by video, and change or cancel it until it starts. You get a reminder the day before.

How do I get the weekly report?

In your parent account. If your child has switched it on, the tutor can also send it by WhatsApp or email.

What about my child’s data?

We collect only what’s needed for learning, never sell it or show ads, and you can ask the tutor, or us, to see or delete it. See the privacy policy.
