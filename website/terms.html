1. What {{brand}} is

{{brand}} helps people prepare for exams. Tutors and their learners share lessons, assignments, tests, marks and messages, and parents follow their child’s progress. Students without a tutor (the student version, in early access) get a plan, lessons, practice papers and mocks, with hints and worked solutions. Prof is the built-in assistant for tutors.

2. Accounts

  • Tutors create their own account. Learners and parents join with an invite from their tutor.
  • Keep your password private. You’re responsible for what happens in your account.
  • Give accurate details, and use one account per person.

3. Age and parents

  • Students in grade 8 and above can start on their own. Below grade 8, a parent or guardian sets up or approves the account.
  • Where the law in your country asks for a parent’s consent for anyone under 18 (for example India), a parent confirms by email.
  • Tutors who invite learners under 18 confirm that a parent or guardian agrees. Parents can have a free, read-only parent account.
  • The early-access list is for people aged 13 or over, or parents signing up for their child.

4. Prof, the assistant

  • With a tutor: Prof works for the tutor. Everything it makes (marks, feedback, plans, reports, questions) is a draft the tutor checks and approves before a learner or parent sees it. The tutor is responsible for what they approve.
  • On your own (student version): students don’t use AI. Lessons, questions, hints and practice papers are prepared by {{brand}} with Prof’s help and checked automatically before they reach you, and you can report anything that looks wrong; reports are reviewed by the {{brand}} team.
  • Prof can make mistakes. Check anything important with your teacher or tutor.

5. Your content

What you put into {{brand}} stays yours. You give us permission to store it, show it to the people you share it with, and process it to run the features you use (for example, sending work to Prof when a tutor asks it to mark). We don’t claim ownership and we don’t sell it.

6. Fair use

  • Only upload material you have the right to use. {{brand}} doesn’t host or share exam-board papers: official past papers open from the exam board’s own website, and tutors use their own copies with their own learners.
  • {{brand}}’s practice papers are original papers written in the format of each exam. They are not official papers. Cambridge, Pearson Edexcel and IB are trademarks of their owners, and {{brand}} is not affiliated with or endorsed by them.
  • Don’t use {{brand}} to harass anyone, break the law, cheat in real exams, or attack or overload the service.
  • We may suspend an account that breaks these terms, and we’ll tell you why where we can.

7. Plans and payment

  • Tutors pay for each learner they teach, on Essentials or Plus, monthly or yearly, at the prices on the pricing page. Students on the student version pay monthly, or once with an exam pass that ends after their exams (see the pricing page). Parent accounts, and the accounts of a tutor’s learners, are free.
  • Every paid plan starts with a {{trialDays}}-day free trial. If you don’t choose a plan by the end of it, your account turns read-only; nothing is deleted.
  • During early access, payment is arranged directly with {{brand}}. When online checkout opens, payments are handled by Stripe (or a mobile money provider); monthly and yearly plans renew until you cancel, and you keep your plan to the end of the period you’ve paid for.
  • You can have a full refund within 7 days of your first payment.
  • We’ll give at least 30 days’ notice before a price change affects you.

8. Your data

You can download your data or delete your account from Settings at any time. The privacy policy explains what we keep and why.

9. Availability

We work hard to keep {{brand}} running and your work safe, but we can’t promise it will never be interrupted. Keep your own copies of anything important.

10. Liability

{{brand}} is a tool to help with tutoring. Results in exams depend on many things, and we can’t guarantee any result. As far as the law allows, we aren’t liable for indirect losses, and our total liability is limited to what you paid us in the 12 months before the problem.

11. Changes

We may update these terms. If a change matters, we’ll tell you in the app before it takes effect.

12. Contact

Questions? Email {{contactEmail}} or use the Contact page. If you already use {{brand}}, you can also write to us from Settings in the app.
