Questions from tutors

Is it really free?

Yes. The Free plan is one learner with every feature, for as long as you like. While paid plans are being set up, nobody is charged. When they start, you choose whether to upgrade.

Do my learners use AI?

No. Prof works only for you. Nothing it drafts reaches a learner or parent until you’ve checked and approved it.

Can I use my own past papers and books?

Yes. Import a whole folder: papers are filed, paired with their mark schemes and kept private to you. {{brand}} never shares exam-board papers.

My learners live in another time zone.

Weekly lessons keep your clock time and show each learner their own, with reminders a day and 15 minutes before.

What do parents see?

Only what you approve: weekly reports, upcoming lessons and due dates, and marks once you’ve returned them. Never messages or working.
