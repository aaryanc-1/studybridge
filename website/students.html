Questions from students

Can I use {{brand}} without a tutor?

Yes, that’s the student version, in early access now. You’ll get a plan to your exam, lessons, practice papers and mocks, with Prof as your guide. Join the list below and we’ll tell you when it opens.

Is it cheating to use Prof?

No. Prof never does your work for you. It gives hints and asks questions so you get there yourself, the way a good teacher would.

Are the practice papers real past papers?

No. They are original papers written to match each real paper’s format: sections, timing, marks and style. Real past papers open from the exam board’s own website. {{brand}} is not affiliated with or endorsed by Cambridge, Pearson Edexcel or the IB.

My subject or exam isn’t listed.

Ask for it on the subjects page. The most-requested come next. You’ll also be able to upload your own syllabus or course outline, from grade 7 to university, and get a plan from it.

How old do I need to be?

From grade 8 you can start on your own. Below grade 8, a parent sets up or approves the account. In some countries the law asks a parent to confirm for anyone under 18, and the app handles that for you.

How much will it cost?

Prices will be announced with early access, with a free way to start. Joining the list is free and doesn’t commit you to anything.
